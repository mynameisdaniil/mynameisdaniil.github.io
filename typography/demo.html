typeset

Justified prose with hyphenation, hanging punctuation and smart quotes: "the quick brown fox" won't jump over the lazy dog...

The TeX algorithm minimises the badness of the whole paragraph rather than filling lines greedily, which matters most in narrow columns like these. Try resizing the window -- the text re-breaks after a short delay.

Add your own fonts and typographic scale; this package only handles line breaking, hyphenation, protrusion and punctuation.
